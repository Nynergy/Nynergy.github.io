CD Rack

Welcome to my CD rack. See an album that interests you? Click on it. I've provided my thoughts on some albums, and marked the ones I love with a ♥.

A. Savage - Several Songs About Fire (2023) black midi - Cavalcade (2021) Boris - Flood (2000) ♥
Even in the moments where Boris eschews the heaviness, they shine. I swear I could listen to Flood II forever. Brian Eno - Another Green World (1975) ♥ Brian Eno - Before and After Science (1977) ♥
Despite this often being considered the weakest of Eno's first run of four solo records, I actually have a huge soft spot for it. It was my first introduction to his non-ambient work, and I will die on the hill that "By This River" is one of the greatest songs ever written. Brian Eno - Ambient 4: On Land (1982) Built to Spill - Keep It Like a Secret (1999) CAKE - Fashion Nugget (1996)
This album was my introduction to CAKE after picking it out of the bargain CD bin at my local FYE. What a fortuitous find. CAKE - Comfort Eagle (2001) Death Grips - Year of the Snitch (2018) Driftless Pony Club - Expert (2009) Driftless Pony Club - Buckminster (2011) Driftless Pony Club - Magnicifent (2012) ♥
I got into DPC after watching a lot of Wheezy Waiter when I was younger. I've listened to every track in their discography too many times to count. "Magnicifent" (yes, the misspelling is intentional) is truly a desert island record for me. Reminds me of all the good times before college, with none of the bad. Driftless Pony Club - Zastera (2016) Explosions in the Sky - Those Who Tell the Truth Shall Die, Those Who Tell the Truth Shall Live Forever (2001) Explosions in the Sky - The Earth Is Not a Cold Dead Place (2003)
Baby's first post-rock record. I'd be lying if I said "Your Hand In Mine" didn't still make me emotional after all these years. Explosions in the Sky - All of a Sudden I Miss Everyone (2007) Harold Budd/Brian Eno - Ambient 2: The Plateaux of Mirror (1980) ♥ Harold Budd/Brian Eno - The Pearl (1984) ♥
While most people flock to "Music For Airports" and "The Plateaux of Mirror" (as they should), I find myself returning to this record the most of all of Eno and Budd's work, solo or collaborative. I could sit within the sound of these landscapes until the end of time. The Killers - Hot Fuss (2004)
This album is so much more than just "Mr. Brightside". So many great tracks that take me back to my childhood/adolescence. lobsterfight - Sun Soaking (2022)
The album that totally sold me on lobsterfight's music. This record is peace, whimsy, and sunshine that makes me feel like everything is going to be okay. The fact that it came from a dark time in Anguel's life only adds to the uplifting feeling of the music. Mogwai - Young Team (1997) ♥
One of the greatest post-rock albums of all time. Heavy, warm, dynamic, and timeless. Nick Drake - Pink Moon (1972) ♥
What can I say about Nick Drake that hasn't already been said? "Pink Moon" is an empty room filled with light and shadow, and it makes me happy to be alive. Pat Metheny Group - Still Life (Talking) (1987)
Shout out to Jojo's Bizzare Adventure for introducing me to Pat Metheny Group. Pat Metheny Group - Letter from Home (1989) The Postal Service - Give Up (2003) Ray LaMontagne - Ouroboros (2016) Saxon Shore - The Exquisite Death of Saxon Shore (2005) Saxon Shore - It Doesn't Matter (2009) ♥
An all-time classic record from a criminally underrated band. Another post-rock gem from my youth that I will go to bat for any day of the week. Songs like "Nothing Changes" and "This Place" are still crushing in the best way, and I will never not get emotional at the closer "Goodnight, So Long". Another desert island album for sure. Another DPC favorite, and best of the pre-DFTBA days. This album is on constant repeat as summer turns to autumn and the weather gets colder.
